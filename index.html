// Rules

Participation Guidelines

Follow these rules to ensure your entry is valid and respected.

01

Original Content Only

The meme or reel must be your own creation. Plagiarism, reposted content, or copied templates without modification will lead to disqualification.

02

Theme Relevance

Content must relate to the given themes (tech life, coding struggles, college chaos, placement pressure).

03

Appropriate & Respectful

No abusive, offensive, political, religious, discriminatory, or explicit content will be tolerated. Maintain campus decorum.

04

Instagram Posting Rule

Participants must post the meme/reel on their own Instagram account and tag the official Stats-O-Locked account.

05

Public Account Requirement

Your Instagram account must remain public during the event for engagement tracking.

06

Engagement Authenticity

Fake likes, bots, paid promotions, or engagement manipulation will result in strict disqualification.

07

Content Quality (For Reels)

Reels should be clear (good audio/video quality) and within Instagram’s standard duration limits.

08

Caption & Hashtags

Use the official event hashtag (if provided) and tag the official account correctly.

09

Submission Timeline

Posts made outside the event dates (21st–27th February) will not be considered.

10

Final Decision

Judges’ decision will be final and binding.
